Refined Clinical Protocol Mapping (CSV-driven)
=============================================

Purpose
-------
This protocol maps CSV rows to clinical substeps based on
Chart_Display and Occluder_State as recorded by the machine.

Key Mapping Rules
-----------------
1) Distance Vision (Step 2.1)
   - Condition: BINO + Snellen/E-chart at the beginning of the CSV
   - Definition of "beginning": any BINO snellen/echart rows that occur
     before the first duochrome row in the file.

2) Right Eye Refraction (RE6.3)
   - Condition: Left_Occluded + Snellen/E-chart/Pictorial
   - Interpretation: Left eye is occluded; testing right eye.

3) Left Eye Refraction (LE6.3)
   - Condition: Right_Occluded + Snellen/E-chart/Pictorial
   - Interpretation: Right eye is occluded; testing left eye.

4) JCC (separate substep)
   - Condition: Chart_Display == jcc_chart OR Occluder_State includes Flip1/Flip2
   - Eye mapping:
       Right_* -> JCC Right Eye (Axis/Power refinement)
       Left_*  -> JCC Left Eye (Axis/Power refinement)
   - Axis vs Power:
       Axis flips: *_Axis_Flip1/Flip2
       Power flips: *_Power_Flip1/Flip2

5) Duochrome (separate substep)
   - Condition: Chart_Display == duochrome
   - Eye mapping:
       Left_Occluded  -> Duochrome Right Eye
       Right_Occluded -> Duochrome Left Eye
       BINO           -> Duochrome Both Eyes

6) Binocular Balance (Step 6.5)
   - Condition: BINO + Snellen/E-chart after the first duochrome row.

7) Near Vision (Steps 7.1 and 7.2)
   - Condition: Chart_Display == near_vision or number_chart_*
   - Default mapping: Step 7.1 (Near Vision Assessment)
   - Step 7.2 (Presbyopia Addition) should be used once there is a
     clear indicator that the patient cannot read near in 7.1.
   - If no explicit indicator exists, keep as 7.1.

8) Pinhole (separate label)
   - Condition: Occluder_State includes Left_Pinhole or Right_Pinhole
   - Label as "Pinhole Check - Left" or "Pinhole Check - Right"

9) Both Occluded
   - Condition: Occluder_State == Both_Occluded
   - Label as "Both Occluded Check (Snellen)"

Notes / Open Decisions
----------------------
- Step 7.2 requires an explicit signal of near-vision failure.
  Provide a rule (e.g., repeated near_vision with same occluder,
  a specific chart switch, or a custom flag) to split 7.1 vs 7.2.
